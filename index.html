C++ · Coding theory · Bachelor's thesis

TC_Toolset — Translation Codes

A modular C++ library implementing the Freiman–Wyner prefix-suffix construction for constrained channels — six modules covering states, FSM, capacity, generation, codebooks, and I/O. Backed by 852 batch experimental runs across parameters d, n, k, r.

  • Key result: rate efficiency η = 99.75% at n=18, k=17, r=4
  • Division factor d shown to have no measurable effect on efficiency
  • Defended with a 12-slide presentation and full speaker notes

Publications & Research

Translation Codes: A Freiman–Wyner Prefix-Suffix Construction for Constrained Channels

Bachelor's Thesis · 2026

Slovak University of Technology in Bratislava — Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Information Technology

Developed and evaluated a modular C++ toolset implementing the Freiman–Wyner prefix-suffix construction for constrained-channel coding. Ran 852 batch experiments sweeping parameters d, n, k, and r, finding a peak rate efficiency of η = 99.75% at n=18, k=17, r=4, and showing the division factor d has no measurable effect on rate efficiency.
